`normalized_levenshtein` is only available for strings. Please add `generic_normalized_levenshtein(a, b)`, mirroring `generic_levenshtein`: it takes two references to collections whose references are `IntoIterator`, with items comparable by `PartialEq` (item types may differ), and returns the score as an `f64` between 0.0 and 1.0 (1.0 for two empty sequences, otherwise 1 minus the distance over the length of the longer sequence).
